No One Stole Your Future.
The Future Was Never Yours.

约 15,000 字的英文三部曲：AI 时代的教育职责、人类能力边界与个人技术主权。已投稿 Aeon Magazine（审稿中）。

PART I

The lack of coming-of-age education in the Chinese education system: A Systematic Inward Collapse of Subjectivity

“This system wraps itself around the child like amniotic fluid — warm, total, and invisible precisely because it is everywhere. Inside it, you forget that you have a self to develop. You forget that the world outside is indifferent to you in ways the system never was. And then, at the moment of university graduation, the same system that spent two decades administering your existence simply releases you — throws you, without warning, into the eye of the storm. It is only then that most people discover what was never built: the skeletal structure of a fully formed person, the bones strong enough to hold up an independent self.”

Excerpt · Trilogy No.1

PART II

No one stole your future, the future was never yours: On subjectivity, optimization, and what AI reveals about the self you were never allowed to build

“What the heir cannot replicate is not the output. It can match the output, perhaps exceed it. What it cannot replicate is the origin — the fact that something was made by a being who did not have to make it, who had finite time and chose to spend it this way, who carried the specific weight of a specific life and pressed it into form. A poem written by something that cannot die is not the same kind of object as a poem written by something that can. Not because one is more beautiful. Because they are not the same act.”
